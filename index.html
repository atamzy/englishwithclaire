FAQ

Questions mums ask

Can I join with my baby on my lap?

Yes, you are welcome. Cameras and microphones can be off whenever you need.

I’m a complete beginner (or very rusty). Is that OK?

Absolutely. Every level is welcome, and nobody judges.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded, so you can catch up when it suits you.

How much time do I need?

2–3 short classes a week, plus a few minutes of practice if you wish.

Can I pause my plan?

Yes. Pause for up to 2 weeks, free of charge.

Privacy policy

We collect only what you give us: your name, email and, optionally, your WhatsApp number and your baby’s age. We use these details only to arrange your classes and reply to your messages. Account details are stored in your own browser, and passwords are saved in hashed form. When you send a booking or message, it goes to englishwithclaire@gmail.com or WhatsApp. We do not sell your data. To have your details removed, email us.

Terms

The first trial class is free. Paid plans are billed monthly, can be paused for up to 2 weeks free of charge and can be cancelled at any time. Class recordings are for enrolled students only and must not be shared. Please be kind and respectful to teachers and classmates. [Final terms to be confirmed by english with Claire.]
